1103 Duel ADS System v4.6.0 CHANGELOG

[ADS-style common legality]
- Gozen Match / Rivalry of Warlords now prohibit illegal face-up Normal/Special/Flip Summons before the summon is committed.
- Face-down monster Set remains legal under those restrictions.
- Common Special Summon lock checks now include temporary turn locks, Vanity's Fiend, Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo and Vanity's Emptiness.
- EX Deck selection, summon procedures, effect Special Summons and flip/normal summon routes re-check common legality.
- Continuous Trap restrictions do not apply while activation is still pending/negated.

[Common state/action support]
- Added explicit specialSummoned field-state tracking for effect/EX/ritual/token Special Summons.
- Added FX_MONSTER_EQUIP_V46 for monster-as-Equip behavior without circular references.
- Equip cleanup now routes equipped monster cards to their owner correctly.
- Added replay-safe FX_CARD_FLAG_V46 explicit state set/delete action.
- graveInfo now retains field level/face and destroying player where available.
