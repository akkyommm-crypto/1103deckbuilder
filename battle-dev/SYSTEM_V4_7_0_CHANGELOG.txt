[Common engine / replay-safe support]
- Added `specialSummonedFrom` and `specialSummonKind` provenance to effect/EX Special Summon state.

- Added `API.unequip()` and strengthened FX_UNEQUIP cleanup for monster-as-Equip cards.

- Added Number 30: Acid Golem controller-only Special Summon lock to the common Special Summon legality gate.
- v4.7 zone-local flags and Special Summon provenance reset when a card leaves the field.
